# Legak business plan

## One-line pitch

Four weeks and 10k EUR yesterday. Three hours today, with an audit trail the
manual process never produced.

## Customer

Primary buyers are recurring deal teams:

- French mid-cap private equity funds.
- Transaction Services teams.
- Social-law firms that sign the final work product.

The first wedge is not replacing counsel. Legak prepares the verdict, the
expert signs it.

## Product

Legak converts an employment data room into a structured due diligence report:

- document classification and request-list generation;
- dated legal parameters and rule-by-rule controls;
- deterministic payroll, DSN, URSSAF, and evidence checks;
- risk qualification, exposure range, and candidate SPA/GAP clause;
- clickable proof chain from conclusion to source passage.

## Market thesis

The current French employment DD market looks narrow because the human workflow
is too expensive for small and mid-cap deals. Legak expands the market by
lowering the price point and making recurring compliance audits economically
viable.

Entry market:

- deals already buying employment DD;
- deals skipping it because 10k EUR and four weeks is too much.

Expansion:

- recurring URSSAF compliance;
- vendor due diligence;
- W&I underwriting evidence;
- Belgium, Luxembourg, then Germany.

## Pricing

- 2.5k-4k EUR per audit for the first productized dossier.
- Annual plan for funds, TS teams, and law firms running repeated deals.
- Premium module for W&I-grade immutable audit trails.

## Defensibility

The moat is not the base model. It is the dated French employment-law rule
base: parameters, expected documents, proof status, calculation functions, and
expert-reviewed anomalies accumulated per dossier.

## Milestones

- Hackathon: reliable replay on provident scheme and vehicle benefit checks.
- Month 1: three design partners on anonymized real dossiers.
- Month 3: first paid pilot on a live deal.
- Month 6: revenue or seed round, plus data-room partnership discussion.
