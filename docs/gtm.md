# Legak GTM

## Positioning

Legak is the audit engine for French employment due diligence. It is not a
legal chatbot, a generic data-room summarizer, or a dashboard. It is a
supervised workflow that produces a priced, cited, negotiable audit report.

## Beachhead

France mid-market M&A, starting with Syntec-heavy services companies where
payroll, provident schemes, benefits in kind, and CSE/Hamon thresholds create
high-frequency risk.

## First design partners

1. One mid-cap fund with five or more French deals per year.
2. One employment-law firm willing to sign the final report.
3. One Transaction Services team that already feels DD delay pressure.

## Sales motion

- Start with a free anonymized replay on one closed deal.
- Produce a delta memo: findings found, findings missed, time saved, proof
  quality.
- Convert to a paid pilot on a live deal.
- Expand to annual usage when the team has two successful deal cycles.

## Demo script

1. Upload/replay data room.
2. Show request list and missing evidence.
3. Run one live arbitration on a legal divergence.
4. Click a red flag and open the exact cited source.
5. Show the proposed price adjustment, specific warranty, or condition
   precedent.

## Objections

Legal monopoly:
Legak is a tool for regulated professionals and expert teams. The signed
opinion stays with counsel.

Confidentiality:
No client data trains models. Dossiers are isolated, logged, and designed for EU
hosting.

Generalist AI competition:
Generalists can draft. They do not own a dated French employment-law rule
engine with proof expectations and payroll invariants.

## Hackathon ask

Win on demo reliability. The repo should show a working vertical, a real rule
engine contract, and a Google DeepMind primitive that the product cannot work
without.
